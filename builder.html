Valentine Gift Maker

Set everything up here, then press Download to get your finished page.

1 Gift box

Pick the colours of the box and the ribbon.

2 How it opens

Choose the 3D animation that leads to the note.

3 The note & messages

Press Enter for a new line in the bigger boxes.

4 Flowers in the box

This is the bottom of the box seen from above. Add flowers, then drag them where you like. Flowers are always kept inside the box.

Tip: flowers added later sit on top of earlier ones.

Quick layouts:
Click a flower to change it.

5 Music & sound

Music starts when the gift is opened.

6 Photos after "Yes"

They appear as a polaroid slideshow. Leave empty for no slideshow.

📷 Click to choose photos, or drop them here
